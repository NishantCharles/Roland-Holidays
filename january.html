Recommended Destinations in January

Here are our top picks for travel this month, hand-curated based on weather, festivals, and seasonal experiences.

Ideal weather, fewer crowds, and unique seasonal experiences make this one of the best times to explore these destinations.

India

January is peak season across India. Cool, sunny days are perfect for Rajasthan's forts and palaces, Goa's beaches, Kerala's backwaters and snowy escapes in Himachal Pradesh.
